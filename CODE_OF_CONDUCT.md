# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise the work, not
the person doing it.

This project is maintained by one person, so this document is deliberately short and
says only what will actually be enforced.

## Expected

- **Argue about the work.** Disagreement is how this codebase got good — several
  decisions in `docs/adr/` exist because someone pushed back. Bring the argument.
- **Be specific.** "This is wrong because X breaks when Y" helps. "This is bad" does
  not.
- **Accept a no.** Some things will be refused on principle rather than on merit;
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) lists them so a no is never a surprise.
- **Assume inexperience before malice.** A first-time contributor getting the
  vocabulary wrong is a documentation problem, not a character flaw.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — including about someone's identity,
  background, beliefs or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: relitigating a settled decision without a new argument,
  demanding unpaid attention, or dominating a thread until others leave it.
- Deliberately wasting a maintainer's time — bad-faith reports, submissions passed
  off as your own work that you have not read, or contributions you cannot explain.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages
— and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to **info@maverickstudios.net**. Reports are handled privately and
in confidence, and you will get a reply.

Responses run from a private word, through a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban
from the repository. The maintainer decides, in proportion to what happened and
whether it has happened before. Where a report involves the maintainer, say so and it
will be handled with someone uninvolved.

We will not retaliate against good-faith reports, and we will not pretend a report
was resolved when it was not.
