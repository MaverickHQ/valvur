## R6.3, `scan` blocks and returns

In-process over live stdio (`tests/test_scan_blocks.py`, the `McpSession` client in
`conftest.py`): one `scan` call returns `state: done` with its generation and summary,
after `notifications/progress` carrying its token and rising values. A client that
cancels the request and goes away, then calls again, is attached: one scan ran, and
both see the same generation. `scan_status` sent during a scan returns that scan's
result, with its own progress.

On the image, e2e: the cancel, budget and stdin probes, `test_mcp_shutdown`'s
client-disconnect test and `test_nothing_outlives`' kill test pass. The kill probe
failed once, on a run whose image bake had timed out fetching Opengrep's release, and
passed three times after the rebuild (57 s, 52 s, 44 s).
