the Linux lane, and the Mac lane is **pending**, held in `tasks.md` §8 for one run over
the landed stack.

## What shipped

- **Path classes** (R20.2, D56). Every path is `source`, `test`, `fixture`, `docs`,
  `example`, `vendored` or `generated`, by whole segments, and every finding carries
  its class as `context` in `findings.json` and SARIF. Fingerprints are unchanged.
- **The sink inventory is inventory** (R20.3, D47a). `dangerous-eval`,
  `dangerous-exec` and `string-built-sql` are marked `inventory`, counted under
  *Sinks to review* and never active. This is the verdict change, as a fix.
- **One class table** (R20.4, D56). A secret where tests, fixtures, docs and examples
  live ranks `low` and stays active. `weak-hash` and `random` are not reported there,
  and `run.json`'s `removed_by_class` counts what was left out.
- **`usedforsecurity=False`** (R20.5). The rule already honoured it; a test holds it now.
- **The corpus judged again** (R20.6). Track 8's baseline rises from 4.2 to 5.9.
