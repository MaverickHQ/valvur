### 1. zizmor against the workflow findings valvur reports today

Unpinned actions come from valvur's Opengrep rule `valvur.pinning.mutable-action-ref`;
write permissions from Checkov's GitHub Actions check `CKV2_GHA_1`. zizmor 1.30.1 (MIT),
`--offline`, matched by file and line:

| zizmor persona | unpinned actions reported | `CKV2_GHA_1` files with `excessive-permissions` |
|---|---|---|
| regular (default) | 85 of 85 | 16 of 18 |
| pedantic, `--min-severity medium` | 85 of 85 | **18 of 18** |

The regular persona misses repository 7's `permissions: write-all` in a one-job workflow
and sinatra's missing permissions block; the pedantic one reports both, at High and
Medium. `--min-severity medium` drops its informational and low code-smell audits
(anonymous definitions, concurrency limits). All sixteen repositories take 1.1 s.
Checkov's `CKV_GHA_7` (workflow_dispatch inputs, four findings on fastify and requests)
has no zizmor counterpart and is outside D8's rule.

**D8 verdict: zizmor is adopted**, `--offline --persona pedantic --min-severity medium`.

### Found by the exit

- **valvur's own workflows failed zizmor.** Repository 6 is this repository at a pinned
  commit, and it held eleven high findings there. Ten were template injections: the token
  and `github.actor` interpolated into `docker login` and `oras login`, and `github.base_ref`
  into a `git fetch`. The eleventh was a release job restoring the name index from any
  earlier run's cache. At `medium`, seventeen checkouts kept their token in `.git/config`,
  and Dependabot had no cooldown. The self-scan gate fails on any of these. All are fixed:
  the values arrive through `env:`, `persist-credentials: false` is set everywhere, the
  rehearsal deletes its tag through `gh api`, the cache is gone because `valvur update`
  pulls the signed index in seconds, and Dependabot has a seven-day cooldown. A test runs
  zizmor from the image over `.github/`. Repository 6's pin moved to the fixing commit.
- **Two scripts were still on the runner R3.9 retired.** `scripts/corpus.py` refused every
  repository, and `scripts/verify-offline.py` failed on a signature. Both now go through
  the Scan Container, with tests. `verify-offline.py` on the rebuilt image prints
  *VERIFIED: nothing left this machine*.
- The e2e suite on the image built from this tree: 48 passed. The tree-hash test failed
  once because a file changed during the run; it passed after the rebuild.
