### 1. zizmor against the workflow findings valvur reports today

Unpinned actions come from valvur's Opengrep rule `valvur.pinning.mutable-action-ref`;
write permissions from Checkov's GitHub Actions check `CKV2_GHA_1`. zizmor 1.30.1 (MIT),
`--offline`, matched by file and line:

| zizmor persona | unpinned actions reported | `CKV2_GHA_1` files with `excessive-permissions` |
|---|---|---|
| regular (default) | 85 of 85 | 16 of 18 |
| pedantic, `--min-severity medium` | 85 of 85 | **18 of 18** |

The regular persona misses repository 7's `permissions: write-all` in a one-job workflow
and sinatra's missing permissions block; the pedantic one reports both, at High and
Medium. `--min-severity medium` drops its informational and low code-smell audits
(anonymous definitions, concurrency limits). All sixteen repositories take 1.1 s.
Checkov's `CKV_GHA_7` (workflow_dispatch inputs, four findings on fastify and requests)
has no zizmor counterpart and is outside D8's rule.

**D8 verdict: zizmor is adopted**, `--offline --persona pedantic --min-severity medium`.

### 3. Trivy's CycloneDX against Syft's

Trivy as valvur runs it plus `--list-all-pkgs`, converted with `trivy convert`; Syft as
valvur runs it. Packages counted by purl, on the thirteen corpus repositories:

| | Syft | Trivy | Trivy as a share of Syft |
|---|---|---|---|
| every component | 308 | 205 | 66.6% |
| without GitHub Actions (`pkg:github`) | 209 | 205 | 98.1% |

Syft catalogues each workflow's `uses:` as a component (99 on the corpus); Trivy does
not. On packages alone the two agree within 2%: Trivy has more Cargo and Go packages,
Syft more Maven ones (gson: 22 against 14). Trivy's pass costs nothing extra; Syft's
took 19.7 s over the corpus.

**D8 verdict: Syft stays.** The rule counts components, and actions are components.
The near miss is recorded for the owner (§8, "revisit a decision"). By D9, an SBOM that
does not come from Trivy's pass is written only with `--sbom` (R4.4).
