# Phase R29, precision that is measured: the record

Begun in a Claude Code cloud session (D61), which built R29.1, R29.2 and R29.5 and stopped
when its credit ran out; finished in a local session on the Mac. R29 builds D65: real-code
precision measured on enough projects to tell one rule from another, each rule's precision
published, what a second engine finds that valvur does not, and a mutation score that may
only rise.

## The corpus (R29.2)

48 projects, from 13: 22 Python, 19 JavaScript or TypeScript, and the 7 others the corpus
held. Each is pinned by commit with its licence, read from its own file, and its size;
every licence is permissive. The checkouts live in the build cache, never in the tree.
Every active finding of a valvur-owned rule or of Gitleaks carries a label with a reason:
172, of which 3 are `tp`.

The wider corpus found five of valvur's own defects, each fixed test-first rather than
labelled: a project tracking a symbolic link refused as a partial Snapshot (aiohttp),
npm aliases reported as hallucinated (hapi, socket.io), three licence misreadings (got,
aiohttp, MkDocs), and RegExp's `exec` read as a shell command (hapi, undici).

Seen in passing and not changed here: the example secrets in fastapi's `docs_src/` and
sanic's `guide/` rank critical, since neither directory is a docs path class (R20).

## Each rule's precision (R29.3)

[`docs/RULES.md`](../RULES.md), generated from `tests/eval/rules-measured.json`: every
shipped rule's true and false positives on track 1, track 2 and the corpus, read from the
Score's own scans. Measured 2026-10-08 on this Mac, `valvur:dev` `sha256:9bd5ec2b8b27`.

- **25 rules**, 21 of valvur's own and 4 vendored from GitLab's `sast-rules`.
- **None of the 16 measured active rules is under D29's bar**, so none was demoted.
  Four sit on it at 0.5: `python_deserialization_rule-yaml-load`, `valvur.javascript.ssrf`,
  `valvur.pinning.mutable-git-ref` and `valvur.python.open-redirect`.
- **The noisiest on real code** hold on the Benchmark's strength: the vendored `random`
  rule, 35 true on track 1 and 23 false on the corpus (0.603), and `weak-hash`, 37 and 21
  (0.638).
- **Five are unmeasured**: the four LLM-output rules and `insecure-yaml-load` have no case
  of their weakness in any of the three. They stay active, and the page says so.

## What valvur misses (R29.4)

CodeQL 2.27.1's default queries, `build-mode: none`, over the 41 corpus projects in Python
and JavaScript or TypeScript whose licence the OSI approves, at their pins
(`codeql-corpus.yml`, run 37842906925). The terms allow it (`scripts/codeql_corpus.py`,
`TERMS`). The cloud's first run, 37309665546, had analysed valvur's own checkout 41 times,
since CodeQL extracted the workspace and ignored `source-root`; the job now makes the
project the workspace.

**183 results, in 19 of the 41 projects; every one at a line valvur does not report.** No
result of either engine sits on a line of the other's. They are counted, not judged: a
result here is CodeQL's claim, not a label.

| CodeQL query | CWE | results | where | projects | as a valvur rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| `js/missing-rate-limiting` | 770 | 15 | examples | socket.io 14, multer 1 | no: needs the whole app's middleware |

certificate validation turned off, a cookie without `secure`, a short key and an old
protocol. Each would ship only by D29's bar, after its cases exist on a track. Nothing of
CodeQL ships: no query, no result, no binary.

(R29.1: 31 of 37 hunks caught).
